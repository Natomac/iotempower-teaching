Teaching Internet of Things (IoT) the IoTempower Way
====================================================

Welcome to an exciting journey of teaching and learning in the Internet of Things (IoT) domain. 
This framework has been developed to support educators and students alike in exploring the world
of connected devices, systems, and the challenges they address.

- **Express Workshop**: 4 hours
- **Intensive Workshop**: 16 hours
- **Full Course**: 60 guided hours, plus 20-40 unsupervised hours

Starting from the 16-hour version, we cover core topics such as storytelling, basic electronics, IoT architecture, machine-to-machine communication, and exploring open-source IoT integration using Node-RED, all within our fully open-source and in-house developed IoT teaching framework—IoTempower.

We advocate for challenge-based education (CBE), allowing students to work with the technology themselves and apply it to real-world problems. This approach enables students to understand IoT's strengths and weaknesses and its potential to address today's challenges in creative, innovative, collaborative, and communicative ways. We foster an inclusive course mindset, promoting storytelling, exploration, and embracing failure as part of the learning process. Emphasizing collaborative team and group work, we encourage critical reflection, with no differentiation between labs and lectures. From the start, students experiment with hardware, programming, and networking aspects of IoT, bridging the gap between the virtual and physical worlds through prototyping.

Course Elements
---------------

The core sessions include:

- **Storytelling and Story-Driven Development**
- **Basic Electronics**
- **Networking and Communication Protocols**
- **IoT Architecture**
- **Exploring Open-Stack Integrations Using Node-RED**

All these are seamlessly enabled within the IoTempower framework. The software used in our course, including IoTempower itself, is open-source and relies on libraries with permissive licenses, making it affordable and easy to replicate. Students can even turn their projects into commercial products without any license restrictions.

We introduce the IoTempower framework early in the class to facilitate rapid prototyping and quick success, keeping students motivated to learn more. Once students have experienced key IoT development tools (after about 50 to 70% of the class), they begin designing and implementing their final projects. Starting from stories, students describe a challenge inspired by a real-world problem and work towards constructing a feasible solution. Guest speakers from industry and academia highlight different problem domains to guide students in selecting and describing challenges for their final projects.

Learning Tools
--------------

We provide a chat server that allows all IoT students to benefit from each other across university and country boundaries. After several trials and discussions with students, we use Discord in the Tartu, Linz, and Regensburg cases and open it for any teacher who approaches us. Other chat environments can work as well but may lose the networking effect with other students and teachers involved in IoTempower.

Additionally, we offer slides, task descriptions, questions, and videos to assist with technical challenges and support discussions. We are continually creating new video material to support upcoming technical challenges. Chatbots of large language models (LLMs) like ChatGPT, Copilot, or Gemini enhance our class learning experience by expediting individual and team in-class research tasks. They also enrich our discussions by allowing us to critically reflect on their answers and suggestions.

In our course, we embrace the idea that hands-on experiences are crucial for the learning process. We provide each student team with a kit containing sensors, actuators, cables, power supplies, a gateway, and microcontrollers. Special attention is given to selecting each kit component, prioritizing affordability and ease of sourcing in most regions worldwide. This approach equips each team with a truly practical experience, enabling them to control every aspect of their network without relying on external resources and services.
